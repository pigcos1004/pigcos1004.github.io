Privacy Policy

KimsFactory handles user information as follows.

Items of Personal Information Collected

KimsFactory collects the following personal information to provide services:

Required Items: Device-related information that cannot identify a specific individual.

Method of Collecting Personal Information

KimsFactory collects personal information through the following methods to provide services:

  • Collected during the execution or use of the program.
  • Provided by partner companies.

Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information

KimsFactory uses the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To help users utilize the services smoothly.
  • To generate statistical data regarding service usage.
  • For surveys and analysis necessary to review and improve services.
  • To notify users of important announcements as necessary.

Sharing and Provision of Personal Information

Device information collected from the service that cannot identify individuals is used as specified in the "Purpose of Collection and Use of Personal Information." In addition, personally identifiable information is not collected and is not shared with third parties.

Notice on the Use of Third-Party Modules

KimsFactory may integrate third-party modules into smartphone applications. This is done to measure advertising effectiveness for paying ad agencies for services placed on third-party media, and to analyze users' service usage. You can find detailed information about the target applications here.

Rights of Users and Legal Representatives and How to Exercise Them

  • The services only collect device-related information that cannot identify individuals. Therefore, requests for information or corrections regarding specific individuals are not possible.
  • If users or legal representatives do not want such device information to be collected, they can delete the service using their device's built-in functions.
  • Personal information terminated or deleted at the request of the user or legal representative is processed as specified in "Retention and Use Period of Personal Information" below. It is processed so that it cannot be viewed or used for any other purposes.

Installation, Operation, and Rejection of Automatic Personal Information Collection Devices

Purpose of Using Cookies

Cookies are used to determine the visitation patterns, usage types, and user scale of the services and websites used by users to provide them with optimized information.

Installation, Operation, and Rejection of Cookies

Users have the right to choose their cookie settings. Therefore, by configuring options in the web browser, users can confirm each time a cookie is stored, allow all cookies, or refuse the storage of all cookies. However, if you refuse to store cookies, you may experience difficulties using certain services that require a login.

※ How to configure cookie settings (for Internet Explorer): Web browser top menu > Tools > Internet Options > Privacy

App Log Analysis Using Google Analytics

Analysis of the user's service usage is conducted through Google Analytics.

Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

In principle, KimsFactory destroys the user's personal information once the purpose of collection and use has been achieved. However, if there is a need to retain it under internal company policies or relevant laws and regulations, the personal information may be stored for a certain period.

Procedure and Method of Personal Information Destruction

KimsFactory does not collect personal information.

Contact for Privacy-Related Inquiries

If you have any questions, complaints, or consultation requests regarding this Privacy Policy, you can report them to the Privacy Officer at (kimsfactory.com@gmail.com).

Amendments to the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be amended. In the event of material changes, the details of the amendment will be notified to users in an easy-to-understand manner prior to the amendment.
